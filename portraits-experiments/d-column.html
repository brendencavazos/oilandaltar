D. Single centred column

One photograph per row, centred and capped around 780px, each at its own proportions. Nothing is cropped and nothing shares a screen. A grey box holds the exact shape of each frame before it arrives, so the page never jumps as you scroll, and the photograph fades in over it.

The opposite instinct to the grids: this treats a portrait as something to look at one at a time rather than scan. It is a long page — 43 photographs — which is the trade. Names sit quietly beneath each frame; they can come off entirely if you want it as bare as the reference.
