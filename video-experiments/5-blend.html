5. Contact sheet → cinema

The section stays quiet: still frames in a grid, exactly like the photographic sections, so nothing moves until asked and a visitor downloads only the clip they choose. Clicking one hands it the whole screen on black, with the arrows, swipe and keyboard already built for the photographs.

Sound is on from the first tap where the browser allows it — you asked for the clip, so it plays. Where autoplay rules forbid it, it starts muted and the button in the corner turns it on. Vertical clips keep their shape; nothing is cropped.
